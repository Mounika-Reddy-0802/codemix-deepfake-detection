Code-mixed audio deepfake detection

Is the voice on this call real or cloned?

Voice-cloning tools imitate anyone from a few seconds of audio, and Indian phone fraud is spoken in code-mixed Hindi-English, the speech English-trained detectors collapse on. This system listens to a call as it happens, scores every few seconds of the caller's voice, and warns the person being called, never the caller, when the voice looks synthetic.

1
Listen

Caller audio arrives from a browser call (WebRTC) or a phone call (Twilio, 8 kHz). Every 2 s the last 4 s are taken; silence is skipped and each window is level-normalised to −23 dBFS, as every training clip was.

2
Score

A wav2vec 2.0 detector with LoRA adapters, trained on code-mixed speech through phone channels, gives each window P(genuine). Scores are smoothed so one odd window cannot flip the verdict.

3
Warn the receiver

Low windows in a row raise a caution, then a warning, on the receiver's side only. The threshold and the ladder were fixed on development calls before any test was looked at.

Measured results

Every number is read from the project's committed result files (hover one to see which). Equal error rate (EER), lower is better. Each result is also tested against a shortcut floor, what eight cheap signal statistics already reach on the same clips.
