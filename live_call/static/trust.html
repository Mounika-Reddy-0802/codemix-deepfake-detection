Every result is tested against a floor

A floor is what eight cheap signal statistics (loudness and its spread, peak, clipping, DC offset, zero crossings, spectral roll-off, high-frequency energy) already reach on the same clips. A result counts only when the detector beats its floor. Equal error rate, lower is better.
